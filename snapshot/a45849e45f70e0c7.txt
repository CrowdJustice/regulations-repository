                <div>
                    <a class="cs-no-underline" href="/rights-and-protections/iccpr/">
                        
                        <h3>Australia's draft Seventh Periodic Report under the International Covenant on Civil and Political Rights (ICCPR)</h3>
                    </a>
                </div>
            </div>
            <div class="row" style="margin-top: 20px">
                <div class="col-12 col-lg-2">
                    <h4>We asked</h4>
                </div>
                <div class="col-md-9">
                    <p><span><span><span><span><span>In June 2026, we sought feedback from interested stakeholders on Australia&#8217;s draft Seventh Periodic Report under the International Covenant on Civil and Political Rights (ICCPR).</span></span></span></span></span></p>

<p><span><span><span><span><span>Australia&#8217;s </span></span>draft report details actions Australian governments have taken in response to the List of Issues Prior to Reporting (LOIPR) provided by the United Nations Human Rights Committee (the Committee). The report covers the period November&#160;2017 to August&#160;2026. It is due to the Committee by 7&#160;August&#160;2026. The reporting process aims to evaluate how Australia implements its obligations under the ICCPR. </span></span></span></p>

<p><span><span><span>The ICCPR reporting process is an opportunity to engage with the Committee on the international stage to highlight Australia&#8217;s ongoing compliance with civil and political rights domestically and internationally<span>, while acknowledging areas for improvement.</span></span></span></span></p>

                </div>
            </div>
            <div class="row" style="margin-top: 20px">
                <div class="col-12 col-lg-2">
                    <h4>You said</h4>
                </div>
                <div class="col-md-9">
                    <p><span><span><span><span><span>We received 17 submissions from a wide range of stakeholders with an interest in Australia&#8217;s human rights issues. This included:</span></span></span></span></span></p>

<ul>
	<li><span><span><span><span><span>individuals</span></span></span></span></span></li>
	<li><span><span><span><span><span>human rights experts</span></span></span></span></span></li>
	<li><span><span><span><span><span>peak bodies</span></span></span></span></span></li>
	<li><span><span><span><span><span>advocates</span></span></span></span></span></li>
	<li><span><span><span><span><span>the legal sector</span></span></span></span></span></li>
	<li><span><span><span><span><span>advocacy providers</span></span></span></span></span></li>
	<li><span><span><span><span><span>academics</span></span></span></span></span></li>
	<li><span><span><span><span><span>non-government organisations </span></span></span></span></span></li>
	<li><span><span><span><span><span>government agencies.</span></span></span></span></span></li>
</ul>

<p><span><span><span><span><span>We thank everyone who took the time to provide us with their feedback.</span></span></span></span></span></p>

        <div class="dss-card">
            <div class="row">
                <div>
                    <a class="cs-no-underline" href="/legal-system/stat-dec-act-review/">
                        
                        <h3>Statutory Review of the Statutory Declarations Act 1959</h3>
                    </a>
                </div>
            </div>
            <div class="row" style="margin-top: 20px">
                <div class="col-12 col-lg-2">
                    <h4>We asked</h4>
                </div>
                <div class="col-md-9">
                    <p><span><span><span><span><span>Between 16 January and 1 March 2026, we held a public consultation on the operation of the <i>Statutory Declarations Act 1959</i> (the Statutory Declarations Act). This was done aspart of a statutory review, which was required under section&#160;16 of the Statutory Declarations Act. </span></span></span></span></span></p>

<p><span><span><span><span><span>The review follows significant reforms introduced through the <i>Statutory Declarations Amendment Act 2023 </i>(the Amendment Act), which commenced on 1 January 2024. Key changes included:</span></span></span></span></span></p>

<ul>
	<li><span><span><span><span><span>the launch of a digital service that enables people to make a statutory declaration online without a witness</span></span></span></span></span></li>
	<li><span><span><span><span><span>permanently enabling the use of electronic signatures and video-link witnessing (while still retaining the traditional paper-and-ink method).</span></span></span></span></span></li>
</ul>

<p><span><span><span><span><span>The consultation sought views on the effectiveness of the operation of the Statutory Declarations Act and whether further changes were necessary or desirable. </span></span></span></span></span></p>

                </div>
            </div>
            <div class="row" style="margin-top: 20px">
                <div class="col-12 col-lg-2">
                    <h4>You said</h4>
                </div>
                <div class="col-md-9">
                    <p><span><span><span>We received 29 submissions to the consultation from a range of stakeholders, including: </span></span></span></p>

<ul>
	<li><span><span><span>community organisations</span></span></span></li>
	<li><span><span><span>peak bodies</span></span></span></li>
	<li><span><span><span>legal practitioners</span></span></span></li>
	<li><span><span><span>private sector organisations</span></span></span></li>
	<li><span><span><span>government agencies. </span></span></span></li>
</ul>

<p><span><span><span>Stakeholders expressed broad support for the reforms, generally confirming that the expanded digital and electronic execution options have improved efficiency and convenience for Australians making Commonwealth statutory declarations. Stakeholders acknowledged that retaining paper-based execution alongside the expanded options is important to ensure accessibility and choice. </span></span></span></p>

<p><span><span><span>Stakeholders also suggested improvements to the Commonwealth statutory declarations framework, such as:</span></span></span></p>

<ul>
	<li><span><span><span>greater education and guidance, including on the digital and electronic methods of execution for the public, to grow awareness and process integrity</span></span></span></li>
	<li><span><span><span>additional functionality for the digital Commonwealth statutory declaration service to enhance user experience</span></span></span></li>
	<li><span><span><span>strengthened alignment with the <i>Digital ID Act 2024</i> to streamline processes</span></span></span></li>
	<li><span><span><span>targeted expansion of the approved witness list to improve accessibility</span></span></span></li>
	<li><span><span><span>Commonwealth recognition of statutory declarations validly executed under state and territory legislation, to encourage greater consistency across frameworks.</span></span></span></li>
</ul>

                </div>
            </div>
            <div class="row" style="margin-top: 20px">
                <div class="col-12 col-lg-2">
                    <h4>We did</h4>
                </div>
                <div class="col-md-9">
                    <p>We used the feedback received through this consultation to inform the final report of the review, <a href="https://www.ag.gov.au/node/7318">Statutory Review of the <em>Statutory Declarations Act 1959</em> Final Report</a>. The report was tabled in Parliament on 14 September 2026 and sets out the review's findings and recommendations on the operation of the Commonwealth statutory declarations framework.</p>

<p>We thank everyone who engaged in this consultation process.</p>

                        <h3>Administrative Review Council Inquiry on Migration</h3>
                    </a>
                </div>
            </div>
            <div class="row" style="margin-top: 20px">
                <div class="col-12 col-lg-2">
                    <h4>We asked</h4>
                </div>
                <div class="col-md-9">
                    <p>Between 28 November 2025 and 27 January 2026, the Administrative Review Council consulted on:</p>

<ul>
	<li>procedures for Administrative Review Tribunal (Tribunal) review of migration and protection decisions</li>
	<li>aspects of the Department of Home Affairs&#8217; primary decision-making processes.</li>
</ul>

                </div>
            </div>
            <div class="row" style="margin-top: 20px">
                <div class="col-12 col-lg-2">
                    <h4>You said</h4>
                </div>
                <div class="col-md-9">
                    <p>The Council received 13 submissions from a range of stakeholders, including:</p>

<ul>
	<li>advocacy groups and research centres</li>
	<li>community legal centres and other legal organisations</li>
	<li>members of the public</li>
	<li>government agencies.</li>
</ul>

<p>Responses identified matters relevant to primary decision-making for migration and protection applications and the special procedures for reviewing those decisions. These included ways to:</p>

<ul>
	<li>improve access to review and support effective participation</li>
	<li>reduce reliance on written requests and increase the use of interviews during primary decision-making</li>
	<li>improve communication between primary decision-makers, the Tribunal and applicants</li>
	<li>adopt a trauma&#8209;informed approach when engaging with applicants</li>
	<li>improve the timeliness and quality of decisions.</li>
</ul>

                </div>
            </div>
            <div class="row" style="margin-top: 20px">
                <div class="col-12 col-lg-2">
                    <h4>We did</h4>
                </div>
                <div class="col-md-9">
                    <p>The Council will use the feedback provided through the public consultation to inform its report and any associated recommendations. We have <a href="/legal-system/arc-inquiry-on-migration/consultation/published_select_respondent">published submissions</a> from authors who gave their permission.</p>

<p>The Council aims to publish its inquiry report by the end of the year. We thank everyone who engaged with this consultation process.</p>
