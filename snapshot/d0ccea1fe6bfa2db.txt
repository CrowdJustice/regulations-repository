<h3>Introduction</h3>

<p>All Australian governments want an effective and nationally coordinated approach to keep children safe and end family, domestic and sexual violence, including child sexual abuse. Governments are working together and with the wider community to respond to how these problems are evolving and act with urgency and care.</p>

<p>In 2026, the Australian Government is consulting on five new action plans that sit under four national frameworks. This includes the Second Action Plan for the <em><a href="https://www.childsafety.gov.au/what-we-do/national-strategy">National Strategy to Prevent and Respond to Child Sexual Abuse 2021-2030 (National Strategy)</a></em>, as well as:</p>

<ul>
	<li>The Second Action Plan for the <em>National Plan to End Violence against Women and Children 2022&#8211;2032</em></li>
	<li>The Second Action Plan and Second Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander Action Plan for <em>Safe and Supported: The National Framework for Protecting Australia's Children 2021&#8211;2031</em></li>
	<li>The First Action Plan for <em>Our Ways &#8211; Strong Ways &#8211; Our Voices: National Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander Plan to End Family, Domestic and Sexual Violence 2026&#8211;2036</em>.</li>
</ul>

<p>We are designing these action plans to work together. This is crucial, as people affected by violence often deal with many overlapping services at the same time &#8211; such as health, justice, housing and community services. They may have experienced multiple forms of abuse or be living with the lasting impacts of past abuse.</p>

<p>As part of this work, the National Office for Child Safety (National Office) within the Attorney-General's Department wants to better understand your views on preventing and responding to child sexual abuse in Australia. Your input will help us develop a Second Action Plan aimed at keeping children and young people safe from sexual abuse and supporting victims and survivors.</p>

<p>If you are interested in participating in consultations related to family, domestic and sexual violence action plans, please visit <a href="https://engage.dss.gov.au/second-action-plan/">engage.dss.gov.au</a>.</p>

<p>We value your responses and recognise that you may have your own lived experience of child sexual abuse. The purpose of this survey is to identify actions governments should take to prevent and respond to child sexual abuse. It does not seek personal details, nor can individual cases be addressed through this process. If you have concerns about a child's safety or wish to make a report, please visit the <a href="https://www.childsafety.gov.au/make-report"><em>Make a report</em></a> page on our website.</p>

<h3>About the <i>National Strategy to Prevent and Respond to Child Sexual Abuse 2021<em>&#8211;</em>2030</i></h3>

<p>The National Strategy is an initiative of Australian, state and territory governments, and was a recommendation of the <a href="https://www.childabuseroyalcommission.gov.au/">Royal Commission into Institutional Responses to Child Sexual Abuse</a>.</p>

<p>The National Strategy has actions that sit under 5 themes. The themes help categorise the different ways governments can address child sexual abuse. The themes are:</p>

<ul>
	<li>Theme 1: Awareness raising, education and building child safe cultures</li>
	<li>Theme 2: Supporting and empowering victims and survivors</li>
	<li>Theme 3: Enhancing national approaches to children who have displayed harmful sexual behaviours</li>
	<li>Theme 4: Offending prevention and intervention</li>
	<li>Theme 5: Improving the evidence base</li>
</ul>

<p>For a more detailed summary of the National Strategy please read the <a href="https://www.childsafety.gov.au/resources/summary-national-strategy-prevent-and-respond-child-sexual-abuse-2021-2030-and-its-action-plans"><i>Summary of the National Strategy to Prevent and Respond to Child Sexual Abuse 2021-2030 and its Action Plans</i></a><i>. </i>You can also find out what has been achieved under the <a href="https://www.childsafety.gov.au/what-we-do/national-strategy#the-national-strategys-action-plans">First Action Plans</a> under the National Strategy by reading our <a href="https://www.childsafety.gov.au/what-we-do/monitoring/annual-reporting">annual reports</a>.</p>

<h3>About the Second Action Plan</h3>

<ul>
	<li><a href="user_uploads/nocs-second-action-plan-consultation-paper.pdf">Second Action Plan Consultation Paper [PDF 500KB]</a></li>
	<li><a href="user_uploads/nocs-second-action-plan-consultation-paper.docx">Second Action Plan Consultation Paper [DOCX 820KB]</a></li>
	<li><a href="user_uploads/national-strategy-second-action-plan-consultation-paper-easy-read.pdf">Second Action Plan Consultation Paper Easy Read [PDF 2.7MB]</a></li>
	<li><a href="user_uploads/national-strategy-second-action-plan-consultation-paper-easy-read.docx">Second Action Plan Consultation Paper Easy Read &#8211; Text only [DOCX 54KB]</a></li>
</ul>

<p>The Second Action Plan is proposed to continue the work already started under the National Strategy and introduce new activities in recognition of the emerging issues and information identified since the commencement of the First Action Plans. We acknowledge the consultations, research and advocacy efforts that have already contributed to the development of the Second Action Plan so far.</p>

<p>Below is a summary of what participants, including victims and survivors and their advocates, identified as actions that are needed:</p>

<ul>
	<li>Expand awareness raising initiatives to improve community attitudes and reduce stigma.</li>
	<li>Improve access to quality support for victims, survivors and their support networks. This includes funding for existing services and peer support.</li>
	<li>Strengthen the skills and capacity of workers across sectors to respond safely and effectively to child sexual abuse, and to support victims, survivors and their families.</li>
	<li>Address the growing problem of online child sexual exploitation and abuse, particularly in light of harms facilitated by emerging technologies including AI. &#160;</li>
	<li>Explore harmful online content, including pornography, as a driver of harmful sexual behaviours and gender-based violence.</li>
	<li>Reform the Working with Children Check system and strengthen national information sharing, including relevant worker and child safety information.</li>
	<li>Strengthen responses to harmful sexual behaviours displayed by children and young people, including centring victims and survivors of harmful sexual behaviours into our responses.</li>
	<li>Explore options to improve access to legal pathways, including civil remedies, for victims and survivors.</li>
	<li>Continue to focus on preventing offending from occurring in the first place, to keep children safe now and into the future.</li>
</ul>

<p>We want to make sure the Second Action Plan improves outcomes for everyone, and that people from all backgrounds are reflected in our approaches. This includes the following priority cohorts:</p>

<ul>
	<li>victims and survivors of child sexual abuse and their advocates</li>
	<li>children and young people and their support networks</li>
	<li>Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples</li>
	<li>culturally and linguistically diverse communities</li>
	<li>people with disability</li>
	<li>LGBTQIA+ people</li>
	<li>people living in regional and remote communities.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Why we need a Second Action Plan</h3>

<p><b>More than 1 in 3 girls and almost 1 in 5 boys have experienced child sexual abuse in Australia according to the <a href="https://www.acms.au/">Australian Child Maltreatment Study</a><a>.</a></b>&#160;This prevalence rate does not include online abuse, which indicates the abuse rates are likely to be even higher. We also know some parts of the problem are getting worse. Rates of <a href="https://www.accce.gov.au/resources/research-and-statistics/understanding-community-research">online child sexual abuse are rapidly increasing</a>, as are <a href="https://www.acms.au/resources/the-prevalence-and-impact-of-child-maltreatment-in-australia-findings-from-the-australian-child-maltreatment-study-2023-brief-report/">rates of harmful sexual behaviours displayed by children and young people</a>.</p>

<p>The impacts of child sexual abuse are significant and can be lifelong. Victims and survivors may experience poorer physical and mental health, as well as educational, employment and economic outcomes. Victims and survivors of child sexual abuse are at an <a href="https://www.abs.gov.au/media-centre/media-releases/child-sexual-abuse-raises-future-risk-sexual-assault">increased risk of experiencing sexual violence later in life</a>. Child sexual abuse has far-reaching impacts that extend beyond the individual, affecting families and communities.</p>

<h3>What we will do with the survey results</h3>

<p>We want to make sure the recommendations to government for the Second Action Plan take into account the Australian community's priorities and concerns. We will be taking the information you and others provide and will publish a consultation report so you can see what we've been told. For details about what sort of information we are collecting, why we are collecting it and how it will be used you can read the <a>Privacy Collection Notice</a>. You must read the Privacy Collection Notice and confirm that you have read and understood it to commence this survey. Further information is also available in the <a>Participant Information Sheet</a>.</p>

This includes how we collect, store, use, disclose, quality assure and secure personal information, as well as your rights to access or correct your personal information. The privacy and security of your personal information is important to us and is protected by law.</p>

<p>Further information about the collection of your personal information is provided below.</p>
</div>

<h4>Community survey to inform the development of the Second Action Plan under the <i>National Strategy to Prevent and Respond to Child Sexual Abuse 2021-2030</i></h4>

<h4>Who is collecting your personal information</h4>

<p>Your personal information is being collected by the Attorney-General's Department (the department).</p>

<h4>What personal information is being collected</h4>

<p>The department is seeking to collect some of your personal information. This may include sensitive information, such as information about your racial or ethnic origin, sexual orientation or practices and health information.</p>

<p>This information is being sought to inform development of the Second Action Plan under the <i>National Strategy to Prevent and Respond to Child Sexual Abuse 2021-2030 </i>(National Strategy).You may use a pseudonym or remain anonymous when completing the survey, application and submission form.</p>

<p>We are also collecting participants names, email addresses, age range, general geographic location, gender, and organisational details (if relevant) and ask if you belong to any of the National Strategy priority groups.</p>

<p>We recommend that you do not include any sensitive information beyond the demographic information requested above. Any information you provide may be shared with other Australian Government agencies where appropriate.</p>

<p>We may also collect your personal information as is reasonably necessary for, or directly related to, the department's functions and activities including:</p>

<ul>
	<li>reporting</li>
	<li>publishing an analysis of survey responses</li>
	<li>auditing</li>
	<li>reviews, and</li>
	<li>improving processes.</li>
</ul>

<h4>How your personal information will be collected</h4>

<p>We will collect your personal information:</p>

<ul>
	<li>Through your participation in the community survey through the department's consultation hub <a href="/">Citizen Space</a>. <a name="_Hlk214279863">The technology for this service is provided by Delib.</a></li>
</ul>

<p>In the event you are unable to submit your survey responses on the department's consultation hub Citizen Space, you may email <a href="mailto:NationalOfficeforChildSafety@ag.gov.au">NationalOfficeforChildSafety@ag.gov.au</a> for assistance.</p>

<h4>Why your personal information will be collected</h4>

<p>We are seeking to collect your personal information for the purpose of:</p>

<ul>
	<li>Engaging with the Australian public on their priorities and concerns relating to preventing and responding to child sexual abuse.</li>
	<li>Gathering valuable insights from individuals and organisations to inform the Second Action Plan under the National Strategy.</li>
	<li>Informing future policy development in consultation with other Commonwealth, state and territory departments.</li>
	<li>Summarising feedback received through the community survey into a consultation report for publication.&#160;</li>
	<li>Inviting participation in future additional feedback sessions, if they become available.</li>
</ul>

<h4>What happens if your personal information is not collected</h4>

<p>If we do not collect your personal information, we will be unable to:</p>

<ul>
	<li>Contact you to clarify any aspect of the feedback you provide.</li>
	<li>Keep you directly informed with updates on the project.</li>
	<li>Invite you to participate in future feedback sessions, if these become available.</li>
</ul>

<p>If you choose to use a pseudonym or remain anonymous, and in future request access to your personal information, we may not be able to identify the relevant information.</p>

<h4>How your personal information will be used</h4>

<p>We may use your personal information to:</p>

<ul>
	<li>Understand priorities that need to be addressed through the Second Action Plan, including those of priority groups and/or individuals and organisations in specific geographical locations.</li>
	<li>Contact you if we have questions about your submission, response to the survey or with outcomes from the community survey.</li>
	<li>Invite you to consultation sessions and facilitate your attendance at these, if they become available.</li>
	<li>Provide advice to Government.</li>
	<li>Produce and publish documents that provide summaries of the themes and feedback provided through the community survey. This may include a summary of the number of people or organisations who participated and will be de-identified.</li>
</ul>

<p>You will have the option to consent to the publishing of excerpts of your submission. This consent is to use quotes provided by you through your survey response in any consultation report we produce. We will redact any sensitive or identifying information before publishing your response. We may also redact information in accordance with the department's Privacy Policy and the requirements of the <i>Privacy Act 1988</i>, where applicable.</p>

<p>We will use your personal information to develop options for the Second Action Plan under the National Strategy. If we would like to use your personal information for another purpose that is not connected to this notice, we will seek your consent again to use your personal information for the different purpose.</p>

<h4>Who your personal information will be disclosed to</h4>

<p>We may also share your de-identified survey response, including your personal information, with other Commonwealth or state and territory departments to inform future policy development related to the prevention and response to child sexual abuse. If we share your survey response with other departments or agencies, we will redact any personal information.</p>

<p>If you provide information to us (including personal and/or sensitive information) that leads us to consider you, or someone else, may be at risk of harm, we may be required to share this information with police and/or other relevant authorities.</p>

<p>We may also be required to disclose your personal information (including sensitive information) to meet mandatory reporting requirements or to report a suspected crime.</p>

<p>Your personal information (including sensitive information) may also be subject to Freedom of Information requests.</p>

<p>We will publish a summary of information provided by participants on the consultation hub <a href="/">Citizen Space</a>, and on the National Office for Child Safety's <a href="https://www.childsafety.gov.au/what-we-do/national-strategy">website</a>. This information will be summarised, de-identified and combined with data belonging to numerous people to ensure individuals cannot be reasonably identified.</p>

<p>With your consent we may publish de-identified excerpts (including quotes) of your survey response. Where applicable, we may also redact other information in your survey response in accordance with our Privacy Policy and the <i>Privacy Act 1988</i>.</p>

<p>When sharing your responses including where permitted your personal information, we will take reasonable steps to ensure that the information is accurate and relevant.</p>

<p>Your personal information will not be disclosed by the department to overseas parties.</p>

<p>If we identify any other reasons to disclose your personal information other than those listed above or permitted by the <i>Privacy Act 1988</i>, we will seek your express consent before doing so.</p>
