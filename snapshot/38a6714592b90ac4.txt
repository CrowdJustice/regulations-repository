A designated person can be an individual, a business or an organisation.  \u003c/p\u003e\n\n\u003cp\u003eThe statutory guidance lists in detail the sanctions that can apply in respect of designated persons, including: \u003c/p\u003e\n\n\u003cul\u003e\n  \u003cli\u003ean asset freeze on their funds and other assets \u003c/li\u003e\n  \u003cli\u003emaking available funds or economic resources to them   \u003c/li\u003e\n  \u003cli\u003edirector disqualification \u003c/li\u003e\n  \u003cli\u003eimmigration sanction (travel ban) \u003c/li\u003e\n  \u003cli\u003eprovision of internet services  \u003c/li\u003e\n\u003c/ul\u003e\n\n\u003cp\u003eUnder this regime, ships can also be subject to sanctions, if they are specified on the UK Sanctions List or have certain connections with Russia or with persons designated for that purpose. \u003c/p\u003e\n\n\u003ch3 id=\"wider-financial-and-trade-sanctions\"\u003eWider financial and trade including in respect of ‘persons connected with Russia’ (note ‘persons’ includes business and organisations) and with respect to land in Russia or in non-government controlled Ukrainian territory.  \u003c/p\u003e\n\n\u003cp\u003eThese sanctions include certain investment restrictions, a ban on loans and credit, and a ban on insurance and reinsurance. \u003c/p\u003e\n\n\u003cp\u003eCertain financial dealings with sanctioned Russian banks and financial organisations, and with the Government of Russia are also prohibited. It’s also illegal to provide them with certain financial services. \u003c/p\u003e\n\n\u003cp\u003eYou must not provide certain professional services to ‘persons connected with Russia’. \u003c/p\u003e\n\n\u003ch3 id=\"sanctions-on-goods-and-services\"\u003eSanctions on goods and services \u003c/h3\u003e\n\n\u003cp\u003eYou must not export or otherwise supply or transfer to Russia, for use in Russia, or to a person connected with Russia, certain goods in these categories (this is not an exhaustive list): \u003c/p\u003e\n\n\u003cul\u003e\n  \u003cli\u003emilitary, \u003cli\u003edual-use goods and technology   \u003c/li\u003e\n  \u003cli\u003einternal repression goods and technology \u003c/li\u003e\n  \u003cli\u003ecritical-industry goods and technology \u003c/li\u003e\n  \u003cli\u003eaviation and space goods and technology \u003c/li\u003e\n  \u003cli\u003eG7 dependency and further goods and technology \u003c/li\u003e\n  \u003cli\u003eoil infrastructure-related goods \u003c/li\u003e\n  \u003cli\u003echemical and biological weapons-related goods and technology \u003c/li\u003e\n  \u003cli\u003emachinery-related goods and technology \u003c/li\u003e\n  \u003cli\u003eRussia’s vulnerable goods and technology (including certain wood products, tools,  machinery components and chemicals) \u003c/li\u003e\n  \u003cli\u003esectoral software and technology \u003c/li\u003e\n  \u003cli\u003eluxury goods \u003c/li\u003e\n  \u003cli\u003ebanknotes \u003c/li\u003e\n  \u003cli\u003etechnical assistance relating to aircraft and ships \u003c/li\u003e\n  \u003cli\u003ejet fuel and fuel additives \u003c/li\u003e\n\u003c/ul\u003e\n\n\u003cp\u003eRelated financial services, brokering services and technical assistance may also be subject to sanctions. Transit controls are also in force. \u003c/p\u003e\n\n\u003cp\u003eYou must not directly or indirectly provide interception and monitoring services to or for the benefit of the Government of Russia. \u003c/p\u003e\n\n\u003cp\u003eYou must not provide trade services including: \u003c/p\u003e\n\n\u003cul\u003e\n  \u003cli\u003eprofessional and business-related services \u003c/li\u003e\n  \u003cli\u003eenergy-related services \u003c/li\u003e\n  \u003cli\u003einfrastructure or tourism-related services (to non-government-controlled Ukrainian territories) \u003c/li\u003e\n\u003c/ul\u003e\n\n\u003cp\u003eYou must not import, acquire from Russia, from a person connected with Russia or from a third country if originating in Russia, certain goods in these categories (this is not an exhaustive list): \u003c/p\u003e\n\n\u003cul\u003e\n  \u003cli\u003earms and related materiel \u003c/li\u003e\n  \u003cli\u003eiron and steel products \u003c/li\u003e\n  \u003cli\u003ecertain metals \u003c/li\u003e\n  \u003cli\u003erevenue-generating goods \u003c/li\u003e\n  \u003cli\u003egold, processed gold or gold jewellery \u003c/li\u003e\n  \u003cli\u003enatural diamonds, synthetic diamonds or diamond jewellery \u003c/li\u003e\n\u003c/ul\u003e\n\n\u003cp\u003eRelated financial services, brokering services and technical assistance may also be subject to sanctions. Transit controls are also in force. \u003c/p\u003e\n\n\u003ch3 id=\"transport-sanctions\"\u003eTransport sanctions \u003c/h3\u003e\n\n\u003cp\u003eTransport sanctions make it an offence to allow Russian aircraft or ships, or specified ships, into UK airspace or ports and create powers to make directions to effect this.  \u003c/p\u003e\n\n\u003ch3 id=\"oil-and-oil-services-ban\"\u003eOil and oil services ban \u003c/h3\u003e\n\n\u003cp\u003eYou must not import or otherwise acquire, directly or indirectly from Russia: \u003c/p\u003e\n\n\u003cul\u003e\n  \u003cli\u003eoil and oil products \u003c/li\u003e\n  \u003cli\u003ecoal and coal products \u003c/li\u003e\n  \u003cli\u003eliquefied natural gas  \u003c/li\u003e\n\u003c/ul\u003e\n\n\u003cp\u003eMaritime transportation of certain Russian oil and oil products is generally prohibited unless it conforms to the terms of the Oil Price Cap (OPC). \u003c/p\u003e\n\n\u003cp\u003eRelated financial services, brokering services and technical assistance are also subject to sanctions. \u003c/p\u003e\n\n\u003ch3 id=\"crimea-and-non-government-controlled-territory-of-ukraine\"\u003eCrimea and non-government-controlled territory of Ukraine \u003c/h3\u003e\n\n\u003cp\u003eSome of the Russia trade sanctions are specific to Crimea and non-government-controlled areas of the Donetsk, Kherson, Luhansk and Zaporizhzhia oblasts of Ukraine. <div class="gem-c-heading govuk-!-margin-bottom-4">

  <h2 class="gem-c-heading__text govuk-heading-m">
    Details
</h2></div>

        <div data-module="govspeak" class="gem-c-govspeak gem-c-govspeak--direction-ltr govuk-!-margin-bottom-0">
    
          <div class="govspeak"><p>The <a rel="external" href="https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2019/855/contents">Russia (Sanctions) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019</a>, and certain other regulations, are in force to meet the UK’s policy objectives. </p>

<p>This summary gives a quick overview of the sanctions in place under the regime. It is not comprehensive and is not a replacement for the statutory guidance or the regulations themselves. </p>

<h2 id="summary">Summary </h2>

<p>The Russia sanctions are broad in scope. </p>

<h3 id="designated-persons-and-specified-ships">Designated persons and specified ships </h3>

<p>The <a href="https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-uk-sanctions-list">UK Sanctions List</a> tells you who is designated under the regime and which sanctions have been applied to them. A designated person can be an individual, a business or an organisation.  </p>

<p>The statutory guidance lists in detail the sanctions that can apply in respect of designated persons, including: </p>

<ul>
  <li>an asset freeze on their funds and other assets </li>
  <li>making available funds or economic resources to them   </li>
  <li>director disqualification </li>
  <li>immigration sanction (travel ban) </li>
  <li>provision of internet services  </li>
</ul>

<p>Under this regime, ships can also be subject to sanctions, if they are specified on the UK Sanctions List or have certain connections with Russia or with persons designated for that purpose. </p>

<h3 id="wider-financial-and-trade-sanctions">Wider financial and trade sanctions </h3>

<p>As well as the sanctions that apply only in respect of designated persons, there are wider sanctions that apply to certain activities with links to Russia, including in respect of ‘persons connected with Russia’ (note ‘persons’ includes business and organisations) and with respect to land in Russia or in non-government controlled Ukrainian territory.  </p>

<p>These sanctions include certain investment restrictions, a ban on loans and credit, and a ban on insurance and reinsurance. </p>

<p>Certain financial dealings with sanctioned Russian banks and financial organisations, and with the Government of Russia are also prohibited. It’s also illegal to provide them with certain financial services. </p>

<p>You must not provide certain professional services to ‘persons connected with Russia’. </p>

<h3 id="sanctions-on-goods-and-services">Sanctions on goods and services </h3>

<p>You must not export or otherwise supply or transfer to Russia, for use in Russia, or to a person connected with Russia, certain goods in these categories (this is not an exhaustive list): </p>

<ul>
  <li>military, security and para-military goods, software and technology and arms, ammunition and related material  </li>
  <li>defence and security goods and technology </li>
  <li>dual-use goods and technology   </li>
  <li>internal repression goods and technology </li>
  <li>critical-industry goods and technology </li>
  <li>aviation and space goods and technology </li>
  <li>G7 dependency and further goods and technology </li>
  <li>oil refining goods and technology </li>
  <li>quantum computing and advanced materials goods and technology </li>
  <li>energy-related goods and technology and infrastructure-related goods </li>
  <li>chemical and biological weapons-related goods and technology </li>
  <li>machinery-related goods and technology </li>
  <li>Russia’s vulnerable goods and technology (including certain wood products, tools,  machinery components and chemicals) </li>
  <li>sectoral software and technology </li>
  <li>luxury goods </li>
  <li>banknotes </li>
  <li>technical assistance relating to aircraft and ships </li>
  <li>jet fuel and fuel additives </li>
</ul>

<p>Related financial services, brokering services and technical assistance may also be subject to sanctions. Transit controls are also in force. </p>

<p>You must not directly or indirectly provide interception and monitoring services to or for the benefit of the Government of Russia. </p>

<p>You must not provide trade services including: </p>

<ul>
  <li>professional and business-related services </li>
  <li>energy-related services </li>
  <li>infrastructure or tourism-related services (to non-government-controlled Ukrainian territories) </li>
</ul>

<p>You must not import, acquire from Russia, from a person connected with Russia or from a third country if originating in Russia, certain goods in these categories (this is not an exhaustive list): </p>

<ul>
  <li>arms and related materiel </li>
  <li>iron and steel products </li>
  <li>certain metals </li>
  <li>revenue-generating goods </li>
  <li>gold, processed gold or gold jewellery </li>
  <li>natural diamonds, synthetic diamonds or diamond jewellery </li>
</ul>

<p>Related financial services, brokering services and technical assistance may also be subject to sanctions. Transit controls are also in force. </p>

<h3 id="transport-sanctions">Transport sanctions </h3>

<p>Transport sanctions make it an offence to allow Russian aircraft or ships, or specified ships, into UK airspace or ports and create powers to make directions to effect this.  </p>

<h3 id="oil-and-oil-services-ban">Oil and oil services ban </h3>

<p>You must not import or otherwise acquire, directly or indirectly from Russia: </p>

<ul>
  <li>oil and oil products </li>
  <li>coal and coal products </li>
  <li>liquefied natural gas  </li>
</ul>

<p>Maritime transportation of certain Russian oil and oil products is generally prohibited unless it conforms to the terms of the Oil Price Cap (OPC). </p>

<p>Related financial services, brokering services and technical assistance are also subject to sanctions. </p>

<h3 id="crimea-and-non-government-controlled-territory-of-ukraine">Crimea and non-government-controlled territory of Ukraine </h3>

<p>Some of the Russia trade sanctions are specific to Crimea and non-government-controlled areas of the Donetsk, Kherson, Luhansk and Zaporizhzhia oblasts of Ukraine. For example the export ban on infrastructure related goods applies only to these areas.  </p>

<p>There are also exceptions specific to these areas, for example  import bans on certain goods may not apply if the goods are certified as originating in Ukraine.</p>
</div>
