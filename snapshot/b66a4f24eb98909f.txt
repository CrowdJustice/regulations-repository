<div class="gem-c-heading govuk-!-margin-bottom-4">

  <h2 class="gem-c-heading__text govuk-heading-m">
    Details
</h2></div>

        <div data-module="govspeak" class="gem-c-govspeak gem-c-govspeak--direction-ltr govuk-!-margin-bottom-0">
    
          <div class="govspeak"><p>This guidance covers the rules for filing accounts each year at Companies House for UK registered companies.</p>

<div role="note" aria-label="Information" class="application-notice info-notice">
  <p>All companies must file annual accounts with Companies House - even if you’re dormant or not trading.</p>
</div>

<p><a rel="external" href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7BoSiuSajWA">File your accounts on time to avoid a penalty</a></p>

<h2 id="how-to-file-your-accounts">How to file your accounts</h2>

<p>You’ll need your <a href="https://www.gov.uk/guidance/company-authentication-codes-for-online-filing">company authentication code</a>.</p>

<p>In the future, you’ll only be able to file accounts with Companies House using commercial software. Read about <a rel="external" href="https://changestoukcompanylaw.campaign.gov.uk/changes-to-accounts/">changes to accounts</a>.</p>

<div class="call-to-action">
  <p><a href="https://www.gov.uk/software-company-accounts">Find software for filing company accounts</a>.</p>
</div>

<p>For now, you can also <a href="https://www.gov.uk/file-your-company-annual-accounts">file your company accounts online</a> or send paper accounts to Companies House by post.</p>

<p><a href="https://www.gov.uk/guidance/closure-of-the-service-to-file-your-company-accounts-and-tax-return">The online service to file your accounts and Company Tax Return closed on 31 March 2026</a>.</p>

<table>
  <thead>
    <tr>
      <th scope="col">Type of accounts</th>
      <th scope="col">Options</th>
    </tr>
  </thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr>
      <td>Unaudited abridged</td>
      <td>Software, Find and update company information</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Dormant</td>
      <td>Software, WebFiling (if not previously traded)</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Micro entity</td>
      <td>Software, WebFiling</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Full unaudited</td>
      <td>Software</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Audited abridged</td>
      <td>Software</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Full audited</td>
      <td>Software</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Small audited</td>
      <td>Software</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Medium audited</td>
      <td>Software</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Group</td>
      <td>Software</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Interim</td>
      <td>Software</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Initial</td>
      <td>Software</td>
    </tr>
  </tbody>
</table>

<p>You can also file certain iXBRL package accounts with Companies House. The accounts must be in a ZIP file format and iXBRL tagged using software. These are:</p>

<ul>
  <li>UKSEF accounts for listed companies</li>
  <li>Welsh accounts with an English translation</li>
  <li>group package accounts - section 400, parent incorporated under UK law</li>
  <li>group package accounts - section 401, parent incorporated under non-UK law</li>
  <li>community interest companies (CIC) accounts</li>
  <li>audit exempt subsidiary accounts</li>
  <li>dormant exempt subsidiary accounts</li>
  <li>limited partnership accounts</li>
  <li>overseas company accounts</li>
</ul>

<p>For full details, see <a href="https://www.gov.uk/file-your-company-annual-accounts">File your company’s annual accounts with Companies House</a>.</p>
</div>
