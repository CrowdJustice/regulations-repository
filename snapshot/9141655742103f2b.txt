      <div class="govspeak"><p>You can order:</p>

<ul>
  <li>a company certificate with certified facts</li>
  <li>a certified copy of a document held on the register</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="how-to-order-and-costs">How to order and costs</h2>

<p>You can place an order by:</p>

<ul>
  <li>using the <a rel="external" href="https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk">Find and update company information service</a> - search for the company, and order from the ‘More’ tab</li>
  <li>calling the Companies House contact centre on 0303 1234 500</li>
</ul>

<p>There are 2 dispatch options for this service: standard or express dispatch.</p>

<p>You should note any <a rel="external" href="https://www.royalmail.com/latest-news">Royal Mail industrial action</a> or other postal delays before you order.</p>

<h3 id="standard-dispatch">Standard dispatch</h3>

<p>The standard service costs £22. Orders for incorporation documents cost £44. We aim to send these orders within 10 working days.</p>

<p>Standard UK orders are sent by Royal Mail Second Class post and international orders by Royal Mail International post.</p>

<div role="note" aria-label="Information" class="application-notice info-notice">
  <p>We’re not responsible for any costs after you’ve received your order, such as courier or legalisation costs.</p>
</div>

<h3 id="express-dispatch">Express dispatch</h3>

<p>The same day service costs £65. Orders for incorporation documents cost £130.</p>

<p>You must place your order before 11am. If you order after 11am, your order will not be sent until the next working day.</p>

<p>Express UK orders are sent by Royal Mail 1st Class post and international orders by Royal Mail International post.</p>

<h3 id="missing-orders">Missing orders</h3>

<p>If you have not received your order, you must <a href="https://www.gov.uk/contact-companies-house">contact Companies House</a> within 28 days of placing your order. After 28 days, you’ll need to place a new order.</p>

<h2 id="including-additional-facts-on-a-certificate">Including additional facts on a certificate</h2>

<p>You can request to include additional certified facts on a certificate. These are:</p>

<ul>
  <li>directors’ names, and details such as date of birth or nationality</li>
  <li>secretaries’ names</li>
  <li>registered office address</li>
  <li>the company’s objects</li>
  <li>summary statement - previously known as the good standing statement</li>
</ul>

<p>The summary statement wording says that a company has been in continuous, unbroken existence since its incorporation and that no action is currently being taken to strike the company off the register.</p>

<p>We’ll only issue a summary statement if the company is up to date with its filings. It must be a:</p>

<ul>
  <li>private limited company with at least one director who’s a natural person</li>
  <li>public limited company with a secretary and at least 2 directors (one must be a natural person)</li>
</ul>

<div role="note" aria-label="Information" class="application-notice info-notice">
  <p>You cannot order certificates with information on people with significant control (PSCs), shareholders, shareholdings or statement of capital.</p>
</div>

<h2 id="legalise-a-document-or-certificate">Legalise a document or certificate</h2>

<p>You can <a href="https://www.gov.uk/get-document-legalised">get a document or certificate legalised</a> with the <a href="https://www.gov.uk/government/organisations/foreign-commonwealth-development-office">Foreign and Commonwealth Office</a> (FCDO). In countries who have signed up to the Hague Convention of 1961, this gives the document the same authenticity it has in the UK.</p>

<p>To get a Companies House document legalised by the FCDO, it must have the original signature of a British public official. To get a document signed in person by one of our officials, you’ll need to request a certified certificate or certified copy.</p>
