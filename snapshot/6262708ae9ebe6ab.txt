    <div data-module="govspeak" class="gem-c-govspeak govuk-!-margin-bottom-8">
    
      <div class="govspeak"><p>You can set up (‘incorporate’) a limited liability partnership (<abbr title="limited liability partnership">LLP</abbr>) to run a business with 2 or more members. A member can be a person or a company, known as a ‘corporate member’.</p>

<p>Each member pays tax on their share of the profits, as in an <a href="https://www.gov.uk/set-up-business-partnership">‘ordinary’ business partnership</a>, but they are not personally liable for any debts the business cannot pay.</p>

<p>You’ll need to:</p>

<ul>
  <li>choose a name</li>
  <li>have a registered address — this will be publicly available</li>
  <li>have at least 2 ‘designated members’</li>
  <li>have an <abbr title="limited liability partnership">LLP</abbr> agreement that says how the <abbr title="limited liability partnership">LLP</abbr> will be run</li>
  <li>register the <abbr title="limited liability partnership">LLP</abbr> with Companies House</li>
</ul>

<p><a href="https://www.gov.uk/set-up-and-run-limited-partnership">Check the rules if you’re setting up a limited partnership</a>. The rules are different for setting up an <a href="https://www.gov.uk/set-up-business-partnership">ordinary business partnership</a> or a  <a href="https://www.gov.uk/limited-company-formation">private limited company</a>.</p>

<h2 id="choose-a-name">Choose a name</h2>

<p>Your name cannot be the same as, or too similar to, another registered company’s name.</p>

<p>Your name must end in ‘Limited Liability Partnership’ or ‘<abbr title="limited liability partnership">LLP</abbr>’. You can use the Welsh equivalents if your <abbr title="limited liability partnership">LLP</abbr> is registered in Wales.</p>

<div class="call-to-action">
  <p><a href="https://www.gov.uk/get-information-about-a-company">Check the Companies House register</a>.</p>
</div>

<p>You cannot choose a name that might be considered the ‘same as’ or ‘too like’ an existing name if it’s too similar.</p>

<h3 id="same-as-names">‘Same as’ names</h3>

<p>‘Same as’ names include those where the only difference to an existing name is:</p>

<ul>
  <li>certain punctuation or a special character, for example the ‘plus’ sign</li>
  <li>a word or character that’s similar in appearance or meaning to another from the existing name</li>
  <li>a word or character used commonly in UK company names (for example, ‘company’ or ‘co’)</li>
</ul>

<p><a href="https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/incorporation-and-names">Check the ‘same as’ rules for punctuation, characters, words, expressions, signs and symbols</a>.</p>

<h4 id="example">Example</h4>

<p>‘Hands UK Ltd’ and ‘Hand’s Ltd’ are the same as ‘Hands Ltd’.</p>

<p>You can only register a ‘same as’ name if:</p>

<ul>
  <li>your <abbr title="limited liability partnership">LLP</abbr> is part of the same group as the company or <abbr title="limited liability partnership">LLP</abbr> with the existing name</li>
  <li>you have written confirmation that the company or <abbr title="limited liability partnership">LLP</abbr> has no objection to your new name</li>
</ul>

<p>You can <a href="https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/incorporation-and-names">read more guidance on naming your company</a> if you’re not sure.</p>

<h3 id="too-like-names">‘Too like’ names</h3>

<p>You may have to change your name if someone complains and Companies House agrees it’s ‘too like’ a name registered before yours.</p>

<h4 id="example-1">Example</h4>

<p>‘Easy Electrics For You Ltd’ is the same as ‘EZ Electrix 4U Ltd’.</p>

<p>If Companies House think your name is too like another, they will contact you and tell you what to do.</p>

<h3 id="other-rules">Other rules</h3>

<p>Your <abbr title="limited liability partnership">LLP</abbr> name cannot be offensive.</p>

<p>Your name also cannot contain a ‘sensitive’ word or expression, or suggest a connection with government or local authorities, unless you get permission.</p>

<h4 id="example-2">Example</h4>

<p>To use ‘Accredited’ in your company’s name, you need permission from the Department for Business and Trade (DBT).</p>

<p><a href="https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/incorporation-and-names">Check which words you need permission to use, and who from</a>.</p>

<h3 id="business-names">Business names</h3>

<p>You can trade using a different name to your registered name. This is known as a ‘business name’.</p>

<p>Business names must not:</p>

<ul>
  <li>include ‘limited’, ‘Ltd’, ‘limited liability partnership, ‘<abbr title="limited liability partnership">LLP</abbr>’, ‘public limited company’ or ‘plc’</li>
  <li>be the same as an <a href="https://www.gov.uk/search-for-trademark">existing trade mark</a>
</li>
  <li>contain a ‘sensitive’ word or expression unless you get permission</li>
</ul>

<p>You’ll need to <a href="https://www.gov.uk/how-to-register-a-trade-mark">register your name as a trade mark</a> if you want to stop people from trading under your business name.</p>

<p>You cannot use another company’s trade mark as your business name.</p>

<h3 id="displaying-your-name">Displaying your name</h3>

<p>There are rules you must follow about <a href="https://www.gov.uk/running-a-limited-company/signs-stationery-and-promotional-material">displaying your company name</a>.</p>

<p>You can trade under your own name, or you can choose another name for your business (known as a ‘business name’). You do not need to register your business name.</p>

<p>You must include all the partners’ names and business name (if you have one) on official paperwork, for example invoices and letters.</p>

<h2 id="registered-office-address-and-email-address">Registered office address and email address</h2>

<p>You must provide an ‘appropriate’ registered office address and email address when you set up a <abbr title="limited liability partnership">LLP</abbr>.</p>

<p>The address you register must be:</p>

<ul>
  <li>a physical address in the UK</li>
  <li>in the same country your <abbr title="limited liability partnership">LLP</abbr> is registered in, for example a <abbr title="limited liability partnership">LLP</abbr> registered in Scotland must have a registered office address in Scotland</li>
</ul>

<p>An address is an ‘appropriate address’ if, in the ordinary course of events: </p>

<ul>
  <li>a document addressed to the <abbr title="limited liability partnership">LLP</abbr>, and delivered there by hand or by post, would be expected to come to the attention of a person acting on behalf of the <abbr title="limited liability partnership">LLP</abbr>
</li>
  <li>the delivery of documents there is capable of being recorded by the obtaining of an acknowledgement of delivery</li>
</ul>

<p>You cannot use a Royal Mail PO Box or equivalent service as your registered office address.</p>

<p>You can use your home address but this will be <a href="https://www.gov.uk/guidance/your-personal-information-on-the-public-record-at-companies-house">publicly available on the Companies House register</a>.</p>

<h2 id="register-your-limited-liability-partnership">Register your limited liability partnership</h2>

<p>You can register your <abbr title="limited liability partnership">LLP</abbr> yourself, or you can <a href="https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/formation-and-company-secretarial-agents">use a formation agent</a> to register your <abbr title="limited liability partnership">LLP</abbr> for you.</p>

<p>We’ll send you a certificate of incorporation when your <abbr title="limited liability partnership">LLP</abbr> has been registered.</p>

<h3 id="register-digitally">Register digitally</h3>

<p>You can use <a href="https://www.gov.uk/company-filing-software">commercial software</a> to register your <abbr title="limited liability partnership">LLP</abbr>.</p>

<p>It’s usually quicker to register your <abbr title="limited liability partnership">LLP</abbr> using software. You can also register your <abbr title="limited liability partnership">LLP</abbr> the same day if you apply before 3pm.</p>

<p>It costs £100 to register your <abbr title="limited liability partnership">LLP</abbr> using software. The same day service costs £156.</p>

<h3 id="register-by-post">Register by post</h3>

<p>Download and fill in the <a href="https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/register-a-limited-liability-partnership-ll-in01">application to register a limited liability partnership</a>.</p>

<p>It costs £124 to register your <abbr title="limited liability partnership">LLP</abbr> on paper.</p>

<p>It usually takes longer to process paper applications sent by post.</p>

<h2 id="members-responsibilities">Members’ responsibilities</h2>

<p>You must have at least 2 ‘designated members’ at all times — they have more responsibilities (for example, keeping company accounts). You can have any number of ordinary members.</p>

<h3 id="making-the-llp-agreement">Making the <abbr title="limited liability partnership">LLP</abbr> agreement</h3>

<p>You should make an <abbr title="limited liability partnership">LLP</abbr> agreement with any other members as part of setting up your <abbr title="limited liability partnership">LLP</abbr>. This sets out how the <abbr title="limited liability partnership">LLP</abbr> will be run, including:</p>

<ul>
  <li>how profits are shared among members</li>
  <li>who needs to agree decisions</li>
  <li>members’ responsibilities</li>
  <li>how members can join or leave the <abbr title="limited liability partnership">LLP</abbr>
</li>
</ul>

<p>You can <a href="https://www.gov.uk/find-a-legal-adviser">find a solicitor</a> to help you prepare an agreement, or you can write your own.</p>

<h3 id="responsibilities-of-all-members">Responsibilities of all members</h3>

<p>Members must carry out their duties and meet their legal responsibilities set out in the <abbr title="limited liability partnership">LLP</abbr> agreement.</p>

<p>Every member must <a href="https://www.gov.uk/log-in-file-self-assessment-tax-return/register-if-youre-a-partner-or-partnership">register for Self Assessment with HM Revenue and Customs (<abbr title="HM Revenue and Customs">HMRC</abbr>)</a>.</p>

<h3 id="responsibilities-of-designated-members">Responsibilities of designated members</h3>

<p>Designated members have more responsibilities than ordinary members and must:</p>

<ul>
  <li>
<a href="https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/self-assessment-register-a-partnership-for-self-assessment-sa400">register the business for Self Assessment</a> with <abbr title="HM Revenue and Customs">HMRC</abbr> — you must also <a href="https://www.gov.uk/log-in-file-self-assessment-tax-return/register-if-youre-a-partner-or-partnership">register separately as an individual</a>
</li>
  <li>
<a href="https://www.gov.uk/vat-registration">register the partnership for <abbr title="Value Added Tax">VAT</abbr></a> if you expect your business’s sales to be more than £90,000 a year</li>
  <li>appoint an auditor if needed</li>
  <li>keep accounting records</li>
  <li>prepare, sign and send annual accounts to Companies House</li>
  <li>send a <a href="https://www.gov.uk/file-an-annual-return-with-companies-house">confirmation statement</a> (previously annual return) to Companies House</li>
</ul>

<p>Designated members must also:</p>

<ul>
  <li>tell Companies House about any changes (for example, to the registered name or address, or members)</li>
  <li>act for the <abbr title="limited liability partnership">LLP</abbr> if it’s wound up and dissolved</li>
</ul>

<p>Designated members can be prosecuted if they do not meet their legal obligations. The <abbr title="limited liability partnership">LLP</abbr> can also be taken off the register.</p>

<h2 id="changes-you-must-report">Changes you must report</h2>

<p>You must tell Companies House about changes to the <abbr title="limited liability partnership">LLP</abbr>’s:</p>

<ul>
  <li>registered or alternative address</li>
  <li>registered name</li>
  <li>members’ details (for example, name or address)</li>
  <li>people with significant control (PSCs), or tell Companies House why this information is not available</li>
</ul>

<p>You can <a href="https://www.gov.uk/file-changes-to-a-company-with-companies-house">file most changes to your <abbr title="limited liability partnership">LLP</abbr> information online</a>.</p>

<p>You can also use this service to tell Companies House which records you’re moving to the alternative address.</p>

<p>Check the list of <a href="https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/companies-house-forms-for-limited-liability-partnerships">Companies House forms for limited liability partnerships</a> for forms you may be able to file on paper.</p>

<h2 id="change-your-llps-name">Change your <abbr title="limited liability partnership">LLP</abbr>’s name</h2>

<p>You must tell Companies House if you change your <abbr title="limited liability partnership">LLP</abbr>’s registered name. You can do this using commercial software or by post.</p>

<h3 id="change-the-name-by-software">Change the name by software</h3>

<p>It costs £20 to change your <abbr title="limited liability partnership">LLP</abbr>’s name using software.</p>

<h4 id="same-day-service">Same day service</h4>

<p>You can use the same day service to change your <abbr title="limited liability partnership">LLP</abbr>’s name (by software only). The same day service costs £85.</p>

<p>You must apply before 3pm. If you apply after 3pm, your application will be processed the next working day.</p>

<h3 id="change-the-name-by-post">Change the name by post</h3>

<p>Download and fill in a <a href="https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/notify-companies-house-of-a-special-resolution-on-change-of-name">notice of change of name of an <abbr title="limited liability partnership">LLP</abbr></a>.</p>

<p>It costs £30 to send a paper application.</p>

<h3 id="use-an-agent">Use an agent</h3>

<p>You can <a href="https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/formation-and-company-secretarial-agents">use a formation agent</a> to register a name change for you. The cost depends on the agent.</p>
