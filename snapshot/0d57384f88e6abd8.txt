    <h2>Overview</h2>
    <p>The&#8239;<a href="https://www.opengovpartnership.org/" target="_blank">Open Government Partnership</a>&#8239;(OGP) is an international, multi-lateral initiative that aims to increase transparency, empower citizens, fight corruption and harness new technologies to strengthen governance.</p>

<p>Australia&#8217;s next national action plan will set out commitments that the government will deliver within a 2-year timeframe to achieve these goals. Australia has had 3 previous national action plans. Our <a href="https://www.ag.gov.au/integrity/australias-open-government-partnership/australias-third-national-action-plan" target="_blank">Third National Action Plan</a>&#8239;(NAP3) ended on 31&#8239;December 2025.&#160;Australia&#8217;s <a href="https://www.ag.gov.au/integrity/australias-open-government-partnership/australias-open-government-forum" target="_blank">Open Government Forum</a> is now developing Australia&#8217;s <a href="https://www.ag.gov.au/integrity/australias-open-government-partnership/australias-fourth-national-action-plan" target="_blank">Fourth National Action Plan</a> (NAP4), to be completed by the end of 2026.</p>

<p>The Open Government Forum is made up of equal numbers of government and civil society members and establishes a space for ongoing dialogue and collaboration between the government, civil society and other non-governmental stakeholders, consistent with the&#8239;<a href="https://www.opengovpartnership.org/ogp-participation-co-creation-standards/" target="_blank">Open Government Partnership Participation and Co-Creation Standards</a>.&#160;It helps make sure commitments in the National Action Plan reflect community priorities and promote greater transparency, accountability and public participation in government decision-making.</p>

<h2>Developing the Fourth National Action Plan</h2>

<p>The Open Government Forum has developed potential commitments for inclusion in NAP4 that meet the following criteria:</p>

<ul>
	<li>Align with open government principles, including transparency, accountability, public participation and integrity.</li>
	<li>Are <b>concrete, specific and feasible</b>, with <b>verifiable deliverables</b>.</li>
	<li>Are achievable within the 2-year implementation period (January 2027 to December 2028), taking into account existing government priorities and policies, legislative settings, resources and implementation constraints.</li>
	<li>Are within the ability of a single Australian Government agency to deliver.</li>
</ul>

<p>Feedback received through this consultation process will be used to inform the priority of commitments and the composition of final commitments included in NAP4.</p>

<p>The potential commitments are:</p>

<ol>
	<li>Delivering Australia&#8217;s First Media Literacy Strategy</li>
	<li>Strengthening Youth Engagement in Government Decision-Making</li>
	<li>Enhancing transparency of corporate ownership to build integrity and trust and reduce the risk of financial crime and corruption</li>
	<li>Strengthening civic participation through collaborative design of model government engagement processes</li>
	<li>Enhancing transparency of AI and digital accessibility</li>
	<li>Strengthening Commonwealth fraud and corruption control frameworks</li>
	<li>Improving access to government information and communications for people with disability.</li>
</ol>

<p>Final NAP4 commitments are subject to Open Government Forum agreement and government approval.</p>

<h2>Who we want to hear from</h2>

<p>We are seeking feedback from all interested stakeholders and members of the public to inform the development of Australia&#8217;s next Open Government Partnership National Action Plan.</p>

<p>In particular, we are seeking views on the importance of the proposed commitments in advancing open government and suggestions for how they should be implemented.</p>

<p>We want to know which of these potential commitments are most important to the public, and for each commitment:</p>

<ul>
	<li>Is the topic of this commitment important to you?
	<ul>
		<li>Why or why not?</li>
	</ul>
	</li>
	<li>How important are the possible benefits of the potential commitment?</li>
	<li>Do you have any suggestions to modify the potential commitment to increase its benefits?</li>
	<li>Do you have any concerns about possible negative impacts from the potential commitment?</li>
	<li>Overall, which commitments should be prioritised for inclusion in NAP4?</li>
</ul>

                function updateQueryStringParameter(uri, key, value) {
                    var re = new RegExp("([?&])" + key + "=.*?(&|$)", "i");
                    var separator = uri.indexOf('?') !== -1 ? "&" : "?";
                    if (uri.match(re)) {
                        return uri.replace(re, '$1' + key + "=" + value + '$2');
                    } else {
                        return uri + separator + key + "=" + value;
