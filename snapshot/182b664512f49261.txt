						<p xml:lang="en">These Regulations make amendments to Schedule 9 (treaty state suppliers (specified international agreements)) to the Procurement Act 2023 (“the 2023 Act”) in order to implement the procurement chapters of two international agreements entered into by the United Kingdom. These agreements are the Agreement on Partnership and Cooperation between the Government of the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland and the Government of the Republic of Iraq signed at London on 14 January 2025 and the Strategic Partnership and Cooperation Agreement between the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland and the Republic of Kazakhstan signed at Astana on 24 April 2024.</p>
						<p xml:lang="cy">Mae’r Rheoliadau hyn yn gwneud diwygiadau i Atodlen 9 (cyflenwyr gwladwriaeth gytuniad (cytundebau rhyngwladol penodedig)) i Ddeddf Caffael 2023 (“Deddf 2023”) er mwyn gweithredu penodau caffael dau gytundeb rhyngwladol yr ymrwymwyd iddynt gan y Deyrnas Unedig. Y cytundebau hyn yw’r Cytundeb ar Bartneriaeth a Chydweithredu rhwng Llywodraeth Teyrnas Unedig Prydain Fawr a Gogledd Iwerddon a Llywodraeth Gweriniaeth Irac a lofnodwyd yn Llundain ar 14 Ionawr 2025 a’r Cytundeb Partneriaeth a Chydweithredu Strategol rhwng Teyrnas Unedig Prydain Fawr a Gogledd Iwerddon a Gweriniaeth Kazakhstan a lofnodwyd yn Astana ar 24 Ebrill 2024.</p>
