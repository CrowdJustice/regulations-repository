      <div class="govspeak"><h2 id="what-this-service-is-for">What this service is for</h2>

<p>This service is for authorised agents (Authorised Corporate Service Providers, ACSPs) to tell us when they have verified someone’s identity for Companies House.</p>

<div role="note" aria-label="Information" class="application-notice info-notice">
  <p>You cannot use this service to tell us your own identity has been verified. You can either use our service to <a href="https://www.gov.uk/guidance/verify-your-identity-for-companies-house">verify your identity</a>, or ask another ACSP to verify you. If you have already verified your identity, you must not verify again unless we ask you to.</p>
</div>

<p>People need to verify their identity for Companies House if they are:</p>

<ul>
  <li>a person with significant control (PSC)</li>
  <li>a director</li>
  <li>the equivalent of a director – this includes members and general partners</li>
  <li>someone who files with Companies House</li>
</ul>

<p>You cannot use this service to tell us about verification checks for the register of Overseas Entities. For this, you should read the <a href="https://www.gov.uk/guidance/register-an-overseas-entity">guidance on registering an overseas entity and its beneficial owners</a>.</p>

<h2 id="who-can-use-this-service">Who can use this service</h2>

<p>You, or the business you work for, must be registered as a Companies House authorised agent to use this service.</p>

<h3 id="what-a-companies-house-authorised-agent-is">What a Companies House authorised agent is</h3>

<p>These are agents that are registered with a UK Anti-Money Laundering (AML) supervisory body. For example, accountants, solicitors or company formation agents.</p>

<p>They must register with us to be able to:</p>

<ul>
  <li>form companies and registrable partnerships on behalf of clients</li>
  <li>file on behalf of clients</li>
  <li>verify the identity of clients for Companies House</li>
</ul>

<p>For more information, read the <a href="https://www.gov.uk/guidance/applying-to-register-as-a-companies-house-authorised-agent">guidance on how to register as a Companies House authorised agent</a>.</p>

<h2 id="before-you-start">Before you start</h2>

<p>You must have completed identity checks that meet the Companies House identity verification standard. These are different to customer due diligence checks to prevent money laundering.</p>

<p>Read the <a href="https://www.gov.uk/guidance/how-to-meet-companies-house-identity-verification-standard">guidance on how to meet Companies House identity verification standard</a>.</p>

<h2 id="what-youll-need">What you’ll need</h2>

<p>You’ll need to tell us the person’s details and information about the checks and documents you used to verify them.</p>

<h3 id="what-youll-need-to-tell-us-about-the-person">What you’ll need to tell us about the person</h3>

<p>You’ll need to tell us the person’s:</p>

<ul>
  <li>name (as it’s shown on at least one of the identity documents they provided)</li>
  <li>date of birth</li>
  <li>home address</li>
  <li>email address - we’ll use this to send their unique identifier, known as a Companies House personal code</li>
</ul>

<p>We’ll include the person’s name in the identity verification statement. This statement will be shown on the online Companies House register when the person connects their verified identity to our records.</p>

<p>You’ll be able to provide a different name if the one on their identity documents cannot be shown on the register.</p>

<h3 id="what-youll-need-to-tell-us-about-the-identity-checks">What you’ll need to tell us about the identity checks</h3>

<p>You’ll need to tell us:</p>

<ul>
  <li>whether the person’s identity documents were checked by a person, or using identity document validation technology (IDVT)</li>
  <li>which documents you used as evidence</li>
  <li>the date you completed the identity checks</li>
</ul>

<p>You do not need to submit copies of the documents you used to verify their identity. However, for relevant documents you’ll need to give the:</p>

<ul>
  <li>document reference number, or equivalent</li>
  <li>expiry date</li>
  <li>country of issue</li>
</ul>

<p>You’ll also need to confirm a statement that you:</p>

<ul>
  <li>are satisfied that the person is who they claim to be</li>
  <li>have completed identity checks that meet the Companies House identity verification standard</li>
</ul>

<div role="note" aria-label="Information" class="application-notice info-notice">
  <p>You must keep records of all identity checks you complete, including copies of the documents. You will need to keep records for 7 years from the date you completed the identity checks.</p>
</div>

<h3 id="print-or-save-a-copy-of-the-details-you-submit">Print or save a copy of the details you submit</h3>

<p>We will show the identity verification details you’ve submitted on the confirmation page. You must print or save a copy of these for your records.</p>

<div class="call-to-action">
  <h2 id="tell-us-youve-verified-someones-identity">Tell us you’ve verified someone’s identity</h2>

  <p>You can access this service from your authorised agent account.</p>

  <p><a rel="external" href="https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/authorised-agent">Sign in to your account</a>.</p>
</div>

<h2 id="what-happens-when-you-verify-someones-identity">What happens when you verify someone’s identity</h2>

<p>We will email the person their Companies House personal code. This is an 11 character code they can use to connect their verified identity to our records.</p>

<p>You must make sure the email address you provide for them is correct. It is the only place we will send their personal code.</p>

<p>They can share their personal code with people they trust to file on their behalf.</p>

<p>For more information about when they will need their personal code, see <a href="https://www.gov.uk/guidance/verifying-your-identity-for-companies-house">verifying your identity for Companies House</a>.</p>

<h3 id="providing-more-information-about-the-identity-checks">Providing more information about the identity checks</h3>

<p>You must provide evidence of the identity checks you completed if we ask you.</p>

<p>We could suspend or stop your business from acting as an authorised agent if you:</p>

<ul>
  <li>cannot provide records of identity checks when asked</li>
  <li>have not met our identity verification standard</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="what-well-show-on-the-public-register">What we’ll show on the public register</h2>

<p>When the person uses their personal code to connect their verified identity to our records, we’ll show their identity verification statement. This will include the:</p>

<ul>
  <li>person’s name</li>
  <li>ACSP name - this is the name of your authorised agent</li>
  <li>names of the Anti-Money Laundering (AML) supervisory bodies the agent is registered with</li>
  <li>date you completed the identity checks</li>
</ul>
</div>
