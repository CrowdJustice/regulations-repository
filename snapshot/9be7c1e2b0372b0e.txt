						<p xml:lang="en">This Order prohibits the importation into Wales of products from susceptible animals (defined in article 2 of this Order) which are intended for personal consumption or use. The prohibition applies to imports arriving from territories which are subject to special transitional import arrangements. These territories include all EEA states, the Faroe Islands, Greenland and Switzerland.
